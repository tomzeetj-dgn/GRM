Privacy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. This page explains the little personal data we do handle, mostly when you contact us or book a session.

Who we are

GRM (GRM Production, GRM Label and GRM Liveroom), based in Belgrade, Serbia, is responsible for the data described here. Privacy questions: info@grmhouse.com.

This website

  • No cookies, no tracking. We don't use analytics, advertising or social media pixels.
  • Settings in your browser. The site remembers your language choice (EN/SR) in your browser's local storage, and keeps a short-lived note during your visit so intro animations don't replay. This stays on your device; we never receive it. You can clear it in your browser settings at any time.
  • Fonts and files are served from our own site. Nothing is loaded from Google or other third parties while you browse.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web host, GitHub automatically processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • External links. Links to Spotify, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Google Maps take you to those services, and their own privacy policies apply once you're there. Nothing from them loads on our pages until you click.

When you contact or book with us

If you email or call us (for example, to book a recording session or a rehearsal), we process what you give us: your name, phone number or email address, your message, and the details of the booking.

  • Why: to reply to you and to arrange and manage your booking. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and performing that contract (Article 12(1)(2) of Serbia's Law on Personal Data Protection).
  • Where: email sent to @grmhouse.com addresses is forwarded through ImprovMX to our team's Gmail inboxes (Google). Bookings are kept in a Google Calendar. The room schedule on this site only shows that a time slot is taken. It never shows who booked it.
  • How long: only as long as needed to handle your request or booking, or longer where the law requires it (for example, accounting records).
  • We don't sell your data or use it for marketing without your consent.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, or ask us to correct it, delete it or restrict how we use it. You can also object to processing, or ask for a copy in a portable format. Write to info@grmhouse.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (poverenik.rs), Bulevar kralja Aleksandra 15, Belgrade.

Changes

If we start using cookies, analytics or embedded media, we'll update this page and ask for your consent where required.
