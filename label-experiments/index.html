Liner notes — inside the gatefold

GRM is an independent record label in Belgrade, working out of the same house as the studio and the live room. We sign artists we believe in, record them properly, and put the record out ourselves. No label-group machinery, no content pipeline — everything on the catalogue was written, mixed and mastered in this building.

Recorded · mixed · mastered in-house — GRM, Belgrade.
