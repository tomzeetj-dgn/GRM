About the label

More than
a label.

GRM is an independent label built around artists, producers and people who care deeply about the work. From the first idea to the final release, we help shape music without sanding away what made it interesting in the first place.

Based in Belgrade, we work closely with our artists across production, visual direction and release strategy — keeping the process personal, collaborative and uncompromised.

02 / The people

Behind GRM.

Different backgrounds. Shared obsession.

  • 01Founder & Creative Director

    Marko Petrović

    Marko founded GRM from a home studio on a simple belief — that small labels can move culture. Years of engineering and production work across rock, electronic and hip-hop taught him how ideas become records, and why the people behind them matter as much as the sound. He still starts most days in the control room before the admin begins.

    As creative director he sets GRM's direction: which artists sign, how the release calendar is shaped, and how each record should feel before a note is even mixed. He also brokers collaborations between producers and the visual team, keeping room for the sessions — and the arguments — that make a project worth putting out.

  • 02Label Manager

    Jelena Ilić

    Jelena grew up in record shops and fanzines, and spent years freelancing across promotion, licensing and tour logistics before it was ever called management. That patchwork background gives her a practical eye for what a release really needs — not just how it sounds, but how it reaches the people who should hear it.

    At GRM she runs the day-to-day machine: release schedules, artist relations, budgets, rights, and the quiet coordination that holds a band's journey together. She's often the first person artists call, and the one who turns a finished master into a live record — catalogue entries, distribution, press and partners included.

  • 03Music Producer

    Nikola Vasić

    Nikola's production style was shaped in small Belgrade rehearsal rooms, then refined over a decade of studio work spanning electronic, indie and hip-hop. He cares about the space between notes as much as the notes themselves, and spends his sessions chasing performances that sound alive even at their cleanest.

    Inside GRM he produces, arranges and records across most of the roster, guiding projects from demo to master while keeping the artist's voice central. He also mentors younger engineers in the studio, building the sessions where GRM's signature — weight, warmth and small, kept imperfections — actually gets made.

  • 04Visual Director

    Sara Jovanović

    Sara moved between photography, graphic design and film before committing to the visual side of music full-time. She shoots covers and campaigns, art-directs releases, and prefers natural light, analogue grain and typography that does its job without shouting. Her work sits somewhere between documentation and design — always with a clear idea behind it.

    For GRM she sets the visual language across everything — album art, portraits, live documentation and press materials — so a record reads like a record from the first thumbnail to the vinyl sleeve. She also oversees the label's photography and makes sure every image matches the same catalogue.

  • 05Sound Engineer

    Aleksa Nikolić

    Aleksa trained in classical recording before falling for the messier world of independent music. His ear is built on room acoustics, vintage chains and long, careful sessions — the kind of patient engineering that lets a take breathe instead of just getting it loud.

    He handles recording, mixing and the final stages of GRM's releases, and keeps the label's technical backbone running. He also works with artists on reference mixes so their rough ideas translate to loudspeakers, headphones and club systems without losing what made them exciting in the first place.
