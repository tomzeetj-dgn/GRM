About the label

More than
a label.

GRM is an independent label built around artists, producers and people who care deeply about the work. From the first idea to the final release, we help shape music without sanding away what made it interesting in the first place.

Based in Belgrade, we work closely with our artists across production, visual direction and release strategy — keeping the process personal, collaborative and uncompromised.
